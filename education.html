The BCC Student Research Scholars Program gives motivated undergraduate students a structured path into translational pediatric oncology research, combining hands-on laboratory work with mentorship, scientific communication, and clinical exposure.

BCC Student Research Scholars Program

Student Research Scholars contribute to active Sholler Lab and Beat Childhood Cancer research projects while learning how discoveries move from bench to bedside. The program is designed for students exploring careers in medicine, biomedical research, biotechnology, or pediatric oncology.

BCC Student Research Scholars Program

1

Undergraduate Research Scholars

Our laboratory also offers the BCC Student Research Scholars Program for motivated undergraduate students interested in gaining hands-on experience in translational pediatric oncology research. BCC Student Research Scholars work closely with scientists and trainees in the lab, contributing to active research projects while learning techniques in experimental design, data analysis, and scientific communication. Scholars also shadow in clinic alongside physicians, including Dr. Sholler, gaining firsthand exposure to how novel therapies and active clinical trials move research from bench to bedside.

  • Laboratory Research SkillsHands-on training in molecular biology, cell culture, genomics, and bioinformatics.
  • Research PresentationBuild communication skills through lab and poster presentations.
  • Scientific Writing & PublicationLearn to write lab reports, manuscripts, and abstracts.
  • Clinical ExposureObserve clinical care and multidisciplinary team rounds.
  • Lecture SeriesAttend seminars and lectures on cancer biology, translational research, and precision medicine.
  • Mentorship & Career ExplorationConnect with mentors and explore careers in medicine and research.

This program is ideal for students considering future careers in medicine, biomedical research, or biotechnology and who want to experience how discoveries in the lab can move toward clinical trials that help children with cancer.

Our mission is to train the next generation of leaders and scientists who will transform care and cures for children and adolescents with cancer through education, mentorship, and discovery.
