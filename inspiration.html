Art in the Lab: Reach for the Stars

In our lab, a series of paintings by artist Joel Schoon-Tanis reflects how families, precision medicine, all the amazing new work being done in oncology, and a community of scientists come together to help children with cancer “reach for the stars.”

Each panel reflects a piece of that story, shaped through conversations with our team. We felt these canvases captured our work, values, research, and science so clearly that we wanted to share them here.

This artwork hangs where we work every day — a visual reminder of why we do this.

A family brings their individual ‘sapling,’ who is then looked at as just that – an individual.”

“By examining the make-up of each sapling, the treatment is personalized to target each sapling in its own way.”

“These different creatures come together to ‘reach for the stars’.”

This panel mirrors our translational teams, where clinicians, scientists, and analysts converge to lift new therapies toward the clinic.

"It is important to note that the flourishing happens as a result of a community working together.”

The intertwined branches echo our lab’s partnerships across Beat Childhood Cancer sites sharing samples, data, and ideas.

“... And that it takes a huge team effort to realize this dream.”

Every study, dataset, and partnership in our lab pushes that shared dream forward and closer to reality.

“In the end, this leads to a grove of flourishing trees—representing a forest of new medical knowledge and healed patients.”

The pieces remind our team that every experiment, dataset, and each scientific collaboration is part of that effort — one small step toward a future where more children can grow up.

Featured art piece

The Promise of Light

by Jorge Roldan (@rojotheartist)

“I believe every extraordinary work of art begins with a story. When I was asked to create a painting in support of Beat Childhood Cancer, I immediately began researching how to best communicate the message I felt was most important to convey—hope and faith.

During the creation of this piece, I paired the painting process with the song “A Million Dreams,” allowing the music to guide the emotion and deepen the narrative unfolding on the canvas. The final composition centers around an angelic figure gently revealing the light of the world to a child, symbolizing guidance, protection, and belief beyond what is seen.

A glowing butterfly emerges as a symbol of the small yet powerful miracles that occur every day. Through the lens of hope and faith, this piece reflects the idea that when we shift our perspective, we open ourselves to positivity—creating space for healing, resilience, and change in our lives.”
