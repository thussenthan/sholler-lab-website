Lab leadership

Principal Investigator

Our team combines translational research, clinical experience, and consortium partnerships to advance precision medicine for pediatric cancers.

Giselle Saulnier-Sholler, MD, MSc

Principal Investigator
gsaulniersholler@pennstatehealth.psu.edu

Pediatric oncologist and translational researcher focused on precision medicine and early-phase clinical trials for pediatric solid tumors. Founder and Chair of the Beat Childhood Cancer Research Consortium.

Research Highlights

  • Advanced DFMO to FDA approval for high-risk neuroblastoma.
  • Chair & Founder of the Beat Childhood Cancer Research Consortium, collaborating across 50+ hospitals.
  • 1000+ citations and h-index 20
  • Led multiple Phase I/II trials in targeted therapy, immunotherapy, and precision-medicine approaches.
  • Key contributor to relapse-prevention strategies improving event-free survival in high-risk neuroblastoma.
